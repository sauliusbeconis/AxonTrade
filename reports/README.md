# Reports

Generated research reports can be stored here when they are small,
reproducible, and safe to commit.

Markdown reports are the preferred durable artifact. Large generated artifacts
should stay outside Git or be summarized in Markdown.

CSV reports are ignored by default. Force-add a CSV only when it is intentionally
promoted as curated evidence and the related Markdown report explains why the CSV
belongs in Git.

`sierra-signal-log-replay-sample.md` is generated from the local replay sample
at `data/processed/AxonTrade_ES_overlay_signal_log_replay_sample.csv`.
