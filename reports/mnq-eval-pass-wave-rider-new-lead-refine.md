## Interpretation

This refinement holds the signal fixed and searches only risk geometry. That is deliberately stricter than changing signal and risk together.

The main tradeoff is not profitability; it is eval survivability. Higher quantity improves two-day pass odds but pushes a single stopped trade closer to the `-$1000` max-loss boundary.

The current practical lead is the highest-ranked row with a planned stop at or below `$800`, not the absolute largest target/stop combination.
