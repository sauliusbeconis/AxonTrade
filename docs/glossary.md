# Glossary

## ACSIL

Advanced Custom Study Interface and Language, Sierra Chart's C++ interface for custom studies.

## Absorption Proxy

A research feature intended to approximate aggressive participation failing to move price through a level. It is a hypothesis feature, not proof of real absorption.

## Failed Auction

A market test beyond or into a reference level that fails to continue and reclaims back through the level.

## HFT

High-frequency trading. HFT-style behavior is prohibited in this project.

## Microscalping

Very short holding-time trading behavior that may violate prop-firm rules. This project avoids microscalping and tracks holding-time assumptions.

## Opening Range

The high and low of a predefined early-session window.

## Price-Only Baseline

A control strategy that uses no footprint or order-flow features.

## Rejected Signal

A candidate setup that did not pass filters or risk checks. Rejected signals must be logged for research.

## Value Area

A volume profile region containing a defined share of traded volume, usually around 70 percent depending on platform settings.

## VWAP

Volume-weighted average price.
