# Vision

AxonTrade exists to become a disciplined futures trading research and execution laboratory, not a shortcut bot.

The long-term aim is to learn whether specific intraday ES/MES and NQ/MNQ hypotheses can survive realistic costs, prop-firm restrictions, replay behavior, and forward simulation. Live automation is only a possible future phase, and only if evidence and safety gates justify it.

## Principles

- Research evidence beats intuition.
- Capital protection beats opportunity seeking.
- Simulation-safe tooling comes before execution tooling.
- Simple price-only baselines come before order-flow complexity.
- Rejected signals are as important as accepted signals.
- Robust parameter ranges matter more than one optimized result.

## Initial Research Domain

The first research domain is intraday equity-index futures, especially:

- ES and MES;
- NQ and MNQ;
- opening range behavior;
- overnight and prior-session reference levels;
- VWAP;
- volume profile context;
- failed auctions;
- absorption proxies;
- stacked imbalance features.

No strategy is assumed to be profitable.
